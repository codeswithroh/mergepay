whoami.app

Link your GitHub account to an Arc wallet once. You can do it before or after your PR is merged: awards wait in the contract until you link. You never need USDC for gas.

.github/workflows/mergepay-link.yml
  1. Pick any repo you own. Your profile repo works.
  2. Add this workflow. Your wallet is filled in once you connect it.
  3. Actions → MergePay link → Run workflow.
