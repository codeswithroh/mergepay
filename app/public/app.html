How a payout happens

A bounty pays when a PR that closes its issue is merged on the funded repo. The pinned workflow asks GitHub for a signed token naming you as the author, and the Arc contract checks GitHub's signature before releasing the USDC. Merges on other repos show here as activity but don't pay.
